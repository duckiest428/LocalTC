# Companion App changelog

Every version of LocalTC Companion for iPhone, newest first. It carries the same version as LocalTC: the app shows
a version's section once, the first time it opens after the update, and the website's changelog page (Companion App)
is built from this file.

## [0.5.0] - Upcoming

### Added
- **Aircraft drawn as what they are** on My Flight: an airliner, a widebody, a regional or business jet, a turboprop, a
  light aircraft or a helicopter, from its type (a Cessna was drawn as an airliner). Your own aircraft too.
- **A new app icon**: a control tower.
- **A weather button on My Flight**: the SIGMETs, TFRs and restricted areas around and the airports' NOTAMs (a badge with the count, a closed runway red) show or hide together.
- **The EFB** (a tab, in place of Airports, which is in it now): the copilot's checklist as it reads it, item by item,
  with Run, Checked, Skip, Hold and Resume; the briefing for the flight's airports (METAR, TAF, NOTAMs, the ATIS); the
  charts from ChartFox (on the PC's Wi-Fi, with your position on the georeferenced ones); the airports; the gate map
  (your airline's gates or all of them, free, occupied, yours or too small: tap one to ask Airport Ops for it); Airport
  Ops (the turnaround, de-icing, closures, messages, notes to Ops); the scratchpad, in step with the app's; and the
  achievements. The checklists, notes, gate requests and notes to Ops work through the account too.
- **My Flight shows ATC's hold and reroutes**: the racetrack with HOLD fix · EFC, a direct or vectors around an area as
  a line, and the TFRs, restricted areas and SIGMETs around.
- **Other flights by their callsigns** with LocalTC's traffic on: their calls and ATC's calls to them look like any
  transmission, under the flight's callsign in its airline's colour, no longer greyed out as "Other".
- **What's new**: after an update, the first time it opens, the companion shows the new version's notes, once.
- **The same version as LocalTC**: the companion now carries LocalTC's version number (0.4.0 for this release
  line), so the app, the phone and the website's changelog all agree.

### Changed
- **Wrapped's icon** is a gift box, no longer bars that looked like a phone's signal.
- **The sign-in screen** shows the control tower logo.

### Fixed
- **The EFB works during a flight.** It said no flight was running: the briefings never came from the PC, and one part of the EFB the app couldn't read emptied all of it. Each part, note, hazard and NOTAM is now read on its own, and one it can't read is left out, not the rest.
- **Your aircraft points the right way when the map is turned**: its icon allowed for the map's own heading.
- **The Live Map's follow button turns off.** Pressing it again stops following the aircraft (and zooming with its
  altitude), and so does moving the map: panning, pinching or turning it. Pressed again, it follows and zooms as
  before.
- **Wrapped goes back** to any week, month or year since the first flight: the arrows no longer stop at "Only the
  last week can be looked back on".

## [0.4.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added
- **Talk to the copilot from the phone**: Comms → Copilot, its answers and callouts in the radio log.
- **Talk to ATC from anywhere**: a call typed in Comms away from the PC's Wi-Fi goes through the account.
- **Themes** (Settings → Appearance), like the app's.
- **The map shows everything the Live Map does**: the ATC zones, the runways, the route, the path flown and the
  traffic, with IFR and VFR maps.
- **Wrapped goes back in time**, week by week, month by month or year by year to the first flight.
- **Replay speeds from 0.5× to 1024×.**

### Fixed
- **The path flown kept its start on long flights**, and before the app was opened.
- **The map across the date line** draws the route the short way.

## [0.3.2] - 2026-09-25

### Changed
- The EFB preview moved to Settings, so the new Logbook fits on the tab bar.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-09-23

### Added
- **LocalTC Companion for iPhone**: sign in with the same email as the app, and the phone shows the map, the
  flight, the frequencies and the radio, with My Flight, Comms (COM1 and COM2, and typing a call to ATC), Airports
  and the Logbook.
