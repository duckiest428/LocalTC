# Marketing

Everything for promoting LocalTC, in one place.

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `brand/` | The logo (`logo.svg`, the source of truth), the app icon (`localtc.ico`, `icon-180.png`), the link preview card (`og.png`) and the GitHub banner (`banner.svg`). Copies: the originals live in `site/`, `install/` and `docs/images/`, made by `tools/make_icons.py`. |
| `screenshots/sim-2026-11/` | 18 shots from flights between 21 Oct and 5 Nov 2026 (Volanta), full size, with 960 px copies in `960/`. |
| `screenshots/website/` | The website's photos at full size (copies of `site/img/`; the site uses its own). |
| `store-listings/flightsim-to/` | The four 1920 × 1080 flightsim.to images, their HTML sources in `src/`, and `render.sh` to remake the PNGs (needs Google Chrome). |
| `videos/` | The promo video, landscape (16:9) and vertical (9:16, for Shorts, Reels and TikTok), and the teaser video script. |

All screenshots are the developer's own flights in MSFS 2024.
