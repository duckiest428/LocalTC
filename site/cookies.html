Cookie policy

Cookies and ads.

Last updated 15 October 2026.

This website now carries Google AdSense, which pays towards developing LocalTC. Its script is on every page, and Google may set or read cookies and similar identifiers on your device through it. Apart from that, the site sets one sign-in cookie if you use the dashboard, and the dashboard keeps a few small settings in your browser. This page lists all of it.

Your choice. Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), you're asked before Google stores or reads anything for advertising, through Google's own consent message, and "no" is a real answer: you then get only non-personalised ads, or none. You can also turn the dashboard's ads off altogether in Account → Ads.

Google AdSense (advertising)

  • Who: Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd., from pagead2.googlesyndication.com and related Google domains.
  • What: cookies and similar identifiers (for example __gads, __gpi, IDE, and the consent choice itself) used to show ads, limit how often you see one, measure them and fight fraud, and, if you allow it, to personalise them.
  • Where the ads show: only on the dashboard: one ad, on every other visit, never more than one on a page, and never on the full-screen Flight Tracker. The script itself loads on every page of the site.
  • Lifetime: set by Google; typically up to 13 months.
  • Type: advertising, not strictly necessary: asked for first where the law requires consent.
  • More: how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and your Google ad settings (to turn personalised ads off everywhere).

Signing in to the dashboard

If you sign in to the optional dashboard, the account server sets one cookie. Nothing is set until you do, and never if you don't have an account.

  • Name: ltc_session, set by api.localtc.tech only.
  • Purpose: keeping you signed in. It holds a random token; the server stores only a hash of it.
  • Lifetime: 30 days, or until you sign out, which deletes it.
  • Type: strictly necessary. HttpOnly (the page's scripts can't read it), Secure (HTTPS only) and SameSite=Lax. It's never used for tracking or shared with anyone.

The dashboard's settings in your browser

The dashboard keeps a few small values in your browser's local and session storage, never sent anywhere:

  • localtc.ads: whether you've turned the ads off.
  • localtc.ads.visits: a count of your visits, so the ad shows on every other one.
  • localtc.ads.noticeSeen: that you've seen the notice about ads, so it shows once.
  • localtc.wrNudgeSeen: which Wrapped reminder you've already seen or closed.

Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

What there still isn't

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, Plausible or anything else counting you.
  • No social or video embeds. Links to other sites are plain links.
  • No third-party fonts. The two typefaces are served from this site.
  • No A/B testing, no session replay, no heatmaps.

Verify it yourself

Open your browser's developer tools (F12, or Cmd‑Option‑I): the Application (or Storage) tab shows the cookies and storage above, and the Network tab shows every request: this domain, Google's ad servers, api.localtc.tech for the dashboard, and the maps' tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org. The source of this site is public, so you can also read it.

Elsewhere

Cookies are somebody else's business as soon as you leave. Clicking through to GitHub or Buy Me a Coffee puts you on their sites, under their cookie policies, and what happens there has nothing to do with this one. Our host, GitHub Pages, handles the request itself — see the privacy policy for what that involves.

Changes

If anything else is ever stored on your device, this page will say what and why first, and anything non-essential will ask you, with a real choice and a real "no". Every edit is in the site's public history.
