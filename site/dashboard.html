Sign in

No password. We email you a link and a code; the first sign-in creates the account.

Open the link in the email, or type its code here.

Creating an account means agreeing to the terms and privacy policy: we keep your email address and the flight summaries you sync, until you delete the account. You need to be 16 or over.

An account is optional. LocalTC works fully without one, and the app keeps your logbook on your own computer either way (the Logbook tab). Signing in to the app copies each flight's summary here: airports, gates, runways, times, distance and the landing. Never your position, voice, transcripts or recordings.
