Sign in

The Flight Tracker, your logbook and support need an account. No password: we email you a link and a code, and the first sign-in creates the account.

Open the link in the email, or type its code here.

Creating an account means agreeing to the terms and privacy policy: we keep your email address and the flight summaries you sync, until you delete the account. You need to be 16 or over.

An account is optional. LocalTC works fully without one, and the app keeps your logbook on your own computer either way (the Logbook tab). Signed in, you get:

  • Flight Tracker: the flight you're flying right now, live on a map, like the companion app.
  • Logbook: every flight synced from the app, with totals and a map of your routes.
  • Support: write to the developer; the answer comes to your email.

Each flight's summary is synced: airports, gates, runways, times, distance and the landing. Never your voice or recordings.

    Support

    What's it about?

    Emailed to LocalTC's developer and not stored anywhere else. The answer comes to . See the privacy policy.

    A flight went wrong? The fastest fix starts with a recording. In the app, turn on Quick Settings → Developer mode, fly it again, press Mark when it goes wrong, then Export session. Say you have one, and the reply tells you where to send it.

    You can also write from the app itself: Quick Settings → Support & feedback.

    Prefer public? Open an issue on GitHub. And if LocalTC made a flight better, a coffee keeps it going.
