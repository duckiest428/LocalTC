How sharing works

Only pilots who choose to

Live Flights shows LocalTC pilots who turned on sharing in the app. It isn't every LocalTC user: sharing is off unless a pilot turns it on, and it needs the free account so it can be stopped at any time, from the app or the dashboard.

Only what's listed

The pilot's display name, callsign, aircraft type, departure and arrival, filed route, IFR or VFR, the phase, position, altitude, ground speed, heading, and the station they're tuned to. Never their voice, recordings, settings or email. Pilots can hide their route and name.

Delayed if the pilot wants

A pilot can delay what's shown by 5, 10 or 15 minutes, so a stream can't be followed in real time. Positions may be delayed; the card says so.

Gone when the flight is

Nothing is stored. A flight leaves the map when it ends, when sharing is turned off, when the app closes, or 90 seconds after its last update. The privacy policy has the details.

Live Flights only shows LocalTC pilots who chose to share their flight. Positions may be delayed.
