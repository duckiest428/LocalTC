Support

Talk to the developer.

What's it about?

It's emailed to LocalTC's developer and not stored anywhere else; your address is only used to answer you. See the privacy policy.

A flight went wrong? The fastest fix starts with a recording. In the app, turn on Quick Settings → Developer mode, fly it again, press Mark when it goes wrong, then Export session. Say you have one, and the reply tells you where to send it.

You can also write from the app itself: Quick Settings → Support & feedback.

Prefer public? Open an issue on GitHub. And if LocalTC made a flight better, a coffee keeps it going.
