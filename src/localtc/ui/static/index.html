New Flight

Imports your latest SimBrief plan. Make one at simbrief.com first. Your Pilot ID is on the SimBrief Account Settings page.

Getting Started

  1. Models. In Quick Settings, pick a profile that suits your computer and press Download for anything not yet installed. Everything runs on this computer; after the downloads, flights work offline.
  2. Flight plan. New Flight imports your latest SimBrief plan, or type one in. ATC clears you to the destination at the cruise altitude, with your callsign.
  3. Start. Load the aircraft in MSFS 2024 (cold and dark or ready to taxi), then press Start. The status turns green when the sim is connected.
  4. Tune and talk. Tune COM1 in the cockpit (or click a frequency above the radio log). Hold your push-to-talk key (Right Ctrl) and speak, or type into the box at the bottom and press Enter.
  5. The flow. Clearance: "Request IFR to Boeing Field". Ground: "ready to taxi with information Alpha". Tower: "holding short runway 34L, ready for departure". Then read back each instruction and check in after every handoff: "Seattle Departure, N172LT, 2,000 climbing 5,000".
  6. Copilot. Flip the Copilot switch and it works the radio with ATC for you: every call, or only readbacks and frequency changes (Quick Settings).

Something went wrong? Turn on Developer mode in Quick Settings. Every flight is then recorded with its audio. Press Mark at the moment it happens, then Export session and send the zip.
